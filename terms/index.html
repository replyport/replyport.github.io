Service description

What ReplyPort does

ReplyPort is a Windows application for Outlook Classic. It can copy mail that a user selects into a dedicated Dropbox App Folder and create native unsent drafts in the corresponding Outlook account. A separate ReplyPort-operated Cloudflare MCP service is being prepared to read approved mirrored records from Dropbox and process draft requests for a connected AI host. The hosted service does not access Outlook; the Windows application performs Outlook operations.

The ChatGPT and Claude directory listings are not verified live, and the hosted service is not approved for real customer rollout. Service details and availability must be updated before any customer launch.

Use of ReplyPort

Use only with authorized accounts and content

Use ReplyPort only with Outlook, Dropbox and AI accounts that you are authorized to use, and only for mail and files that you may process through the selected services. Check your employer, school or other organization’s rules before copying mail to Dropbox or making it available to an AI host.

You choose which mailboxes and folders to copy, whether to include eligible attachments, and which external AI host to connect. Review and authorize each account connection and requested permission.

Draft behavior

ReplyPort does not send email

ReplyPort has no email-sending capability. The Windows application may create an unsent Outlook draft after it validates an authorized request. You must review the draft in Outlook Classic and decide whether to send it manually. A request placed in Dropbox is not a completed draft; only the matching local receipt with a created status confirms that the draft was created.

AI-generated content can be wrong or incomplete. Review the recipients and content before using a draft.

Third-party services

Dropbox, Cloudflare and your AI host

Dropbox stores and synchronizes the ReplyPort App Folder and provides the Dropbox authorization used by the optional hosted connector. Cloudflare operates the hosted MCP service. The ChatGPT, Claude or other AI host you select processes content returned through its connector. Microsoft and your mailbox provider continue to operate Outlook and your mailbox. Each provider has its own terms and privacy information.

Processing and retention details are described in the Privacy policy. The provider terms, platform availability, administrator controls and account requirements may apply to your use.

Privacy and support

Privacy, disconnection and help

The Privacy policy describes current data flows, service providers, retention and logging practices, and user controls. Disconnecting an AI host, revoking Dropbox access, deleting Dropbox data, and removing Outlook drafts are separate actions; ReplyPort does not promise that one action performs the others.

For setup and troubleshooting, see connector setup and support.
