Three jobs across your accounts.

Ask in ordinary language. The examples below use ChatGPT with a university account and a personal account; Claude works the same way.

Data flow

What runs on your PC, and what does not.

ReplyPort’s connection to Outlook runs on your PC. The mail your AI reads does not stay there: the current version copies it to your Dropbox so your AI can reach it.

On your Windows PC

  • ReplyPort works with Outlook Classic directly. No Outlook add-in, and you do not sign ReplyPort in through Microsoft Graph.
  • Every draft request is checked against the live mailbox before a draft is saved.
  • Account bindings and keys stay on the PC. Logs contain no subjects, bodies or addresses.
  • There is no ReplyPort server and no send function.

In your Dropbox

  • Copies of the last 90 days of mail, only from the mailboxes and folders you approve. Mail copying is off until you turn it on.
  • Attachments only if you turn them on for that mailbox.
  • Search and draft requests passing between your AI and the PC.
  • Dropbox’s terms and privacy policy apply.

With your AI

  • Your assistant reads the approved copies and writes requests. It never connects to Outlook or to your PC.
  • Messages marked Personal, Private or Confidential, and encrypted messages, are not copied.
  • Your AI provider’s terms and data policies apply.

Requirements & fit

Check the fit before you buy.

ReplyPort is early-access Windows software. These are the requirements and limits of the current Microsoft Store release.

You need

  • Windows 11 on an x64 PC where you can install apps from the Microsoft Store.
  • Outlook Classic with your accounts already set up. It must be open for drafts to be created.
  • The Dropbox desktop app, signed in on the same PC.
  • An AI assistant that can read and create files in your Dropbox, such as ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, Codex, Claude, Claude Cowork or Claude Code.

Accounts and mail

  • Tested with Microsoft 365 work or school accounts and personal Outlook.com accounts in Outlook Classic. IMAP, POP and on-premises Exchange accounts have not been verified.
  • Searches cover the last 90 days. With your approval, ReplyPort copies all ordinary mail folders of a mailbox; Deleted Items, Junk Email, Drafts, Outbox, shared mailboxes, PST files and Online Archive are not copied.
  • Shared or delegated mailboxes are not included unless they are set up as their own account.
  • Replies, Reply All and new messages, with files your AI chooses attached. No Forward.

Not a fit if

  • You use New Outlook, Outlook on the web, a Mac or a phone only.
  • You want email sent automatically.
  • You cannot install software on the PC, or may not use Dropbox or an external AI with this mail.
  • You need centrally managed deployment for a team.

ReplyPort does not need an Outlook add-in, and you do not authorise it through Microsoft Graph. Your organisation’s device, storage and AI-use policies still apply.

Questions

When ReplyPort is the right tool.

Already happy with Claude for Outlook, ChatGPT’s Outlook apps or Microsoft 365 Copilot? Keep using them where they support your accounts, your organisation permits the connection and they handle the work you need.

ReplyPort is for people who keep several accounts in Outlook Classic, want to use the AI they already prefer, do not want an Outlook add-in, and want drafts created through the Outlook accounts already on their PC.

Is ReplyPort a Claude or ChatGPT product?

No. ReplyPort is independent software and does not contain an AI model. It works with assistants that can read and create files in your Dropbox, including Claude and ChatGPT.

Does my email stay on my PC?

Not all of it. ReplyPort runs on your PC, but so that your AI can read mail it copies recent mail from the mailboxes you approve to your Dropbox. Dropbox and your AI provider process those copies under their own terms. See Data flow.

Can ReplyPort send email?

No. ReplyPort has no send function of any kind. It does not use Outlook’s Send, SMTP, Microsoft Graph or any other mail service. Every draft stays unsent until you send it in Outlook.

How does it choose the account for a draft?

A reply is always created in the account that received the original message. A new message is created in the account named in the request; if the account is unclear, your AI should ask you. ReplyPort never guesses an account from an address or domain.

Do I need another AI subscription?

No subscription comes from ReplyPort. You use your own assistant, on whatever plan lets it read and create files in Dropbox. ReplyPort is a one-time purchase.

Does it work with New Outlook or on a Mac?

No. ReplyPort works only with Outlook Classic on Windows. If New Outlook is active, draft requests are refused.

What does it cost?

US$29.99 as a one-time purchase in the Microsoft Store during early access. Outside the United States, the Store shows the price in your local currency.
