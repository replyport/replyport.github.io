How it works

Ask your AI. Review in Outlook. You send.

ReplyPort never sends email.

Every draft waits for you in Outlook.

  1. 01

    Ask your AI.

    Ask it to find email, summarise a conversation or write a reply. It works with copies of the mail you approve, across the Outlook accounts you enable.

  2. 02

    ReplyPort creates the draft.

    On your Windows PC, ReplyPort checks the account, conversation and recipients in Outlook Classic, then saves the AI-written text as an ordinary draft in the right account.

  3. 03

    You review and send.

    Open the draft in Outlook. Read it, edit it, discard it or press Send yourself.

Requirements & setup

What you need to get started.

No Outlook add-in or cloud mailbox connection required.

Windows and Outlook Classic

  • A Windows PC with Outlook Classic and your accounts already configured.
  • Outlook Classic open when ReplyPort creates a draft.
  • An AI assistant with access to your ReplyPort folder.

Use only mail and AI services allowed by your organisation’s software and data-use policies.
