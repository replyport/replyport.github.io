How it works

Three parts, and one of them is you.

The AI understands and writes. ReplyPort does the mailbox work locally and checks it. Sending is the part that never leaves your hands.

The AI product

Reads and reasons

Works over the mail you approved for mirroring: searching, ranking, summarising and writing reply text.

Cannot reach Outlook, choose an account or address a recipient.

ReplyPort, locally

Routes and verifies

Resolves the mailbox by exact identity, rebuilds recipients and threading from Outlook itself, creates the draft and confirms it landed correctly.

Runs in its own process on your Windows PC.

You, in Outlook

Reviews and sends

The draft is an ordinary Outlook draft in the right account. Edit it, discard it or send it exactly as you always have.

The only step that can put mail on the wire.

The trust boundary

What the AI, ReplyPort and you can each do
Capability AI ReplyPort You
Search and reason over mail you have approved Yes Yes Yes
Work across several configured accounts at once Yes Yes, per mailbox Yes
Compose the reply text Yes No Yes
Choose the account, recipients and thread No Yes, from Outlook Yes
Write and verify the draft in Outlook No Yes, locally Yes
Send No No Only you

“Yes, locally” — the mailbox-changing work happens in the ReplyPort process on your machine, against the Outlook profile already signed in there.

“Yes, per mailbox” — each configured account is resolved separately, and a draft is checked against the thread and mailbox it belongs to before it appears.

Provider-neutral — nothing in the Outlook-side design depends on which AI product you use, so changing surfaces does not move the boundary above.
