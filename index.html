Why ReplyPort exists

Your work mail can already be in Outlook even when the AI tools you use have no direct route into it.

ReplyPort does not depend on an Outlook add-in or a cloud mailbox integration. It runs beside Outlook Classic on your Windows PC and gives the AI product you already use a controlled way to work with the mail you choose. Your organisation's normal software and data-use policies still apply.

No Outlook add-in required
ReplyPort is a Windows app, not an Outlook ribbon plugin.
Multiple accounts, kept separate
Work across several configured Outlook mailboxes without mixing their routing.
Stay inside your AI product
Use ReplyPort from supported ChatGPT and Claude surfaces instead of moving email work into a separate ReplyPort chat.

The jobs that make email feel expensive.

ReplyPort is most useful when the problem is not writing one email. It is finding the right work across a lot of email, then getting through it quickly.

The trust boundary

AI can do the email work. It still doesn't get the Send button.

The convenience comes from search, triage and drafting. Sending remains a human action.

What the AI, ReplyPort and you can each do
Capability AI ReplyPort You
Search and reason over mail you allow Yes Yes Yes
Compose draft text Yes No Yes
Write and verify the draft in Outlook No Yes, locally Yes
Send No No Only you

“Yes, locally” means the mailbox-changing work happens in the ReplyPort process on your machine, against the Outlook profile already signed in there.

ReplyPort resolves each configured mailbox separately and checks drafts against the thread and mailbox they belong to before they appear in Outlook.

Your AI product, ReplyPort and Outlook each keep their own job.

ReplyPort is the local execution layer between the AI surface you already use and the Outlook profile already on your PC.
