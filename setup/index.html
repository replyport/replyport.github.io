ReplyPort · Setup guide

Connect your AI in three steps.

Before you start

  • Install ReplyPort from the Microsoft Store.
  • Open Outlook Classic.
  • Enable mail copying for your chosen accounts in ReplyPort Control Centre.

Connect your AI

  1. Make the ReplyPort folder available to your AI

    Use your AI’s file or Dropbox connection to give it access to /ReplyPort. Installing Dropbox on your PC alone does not give your AI access.

  2. Copy the setup prompt

    Open ReplyPort Control Centre and choose Copy setup prompt.

  3. Paste it into your AI chat

    Follow the guide to save the permanent ReplyPort instructions and run the search test. When it reports Ready, you can ask about your mail.

The setup test searches only; it creates no draft. ReplyPort never sends email. You review and send in Outlook.

If setup stops

Cannot see /ReplyPort
Make the ReplyPort Dropbox folder available to that AI, then paste the setup prompt again.
Mail copying is off
Open ReplyPort Control Centre and enable mail copying for at least one mailbox, then continue setup.
Setup test times out
Check that the PC is on, the ReplyPort tray icon is running, Outlook Classic is open and Dropbox is syncing, then ask the AI to run the ReplyPort setup test again.
